# Your own files

## In short

Pictures you made yourself, or that are yours to keep. There are no external terms; what you do with them is up to you.

## You may

- Use them as wallpapers and do anything else you like with your own work.

## You must

- Check that the file really is yours (or that you have the right to use it) before publishing it.

## You cannot

- Assume that other people may reuse them: you have not granted anyone a license.

## In this collection

Download-only: files under this license are not committed; wallman keeps them on your disk, records where to fetch them, and never adds them to git. wallman never commits personal files automatically; use the repository's own rules if you want to publish one.
