# Studio Ghibli free-use stills

## In short

Studio Ghibli publishes selected film stills on its website for free use and asks that they are used with common sense. The studio keeps the copyright.

## You may

- Use the stills the studio published for this purpose, for example as a desktop background.

## You must

- Follow the terms on the official page, which the studio can change; check it before using a still in any public way.
- Use them with common sense, as the studio asks.

## You cannot

- Use them to harm the studio or its characters, or in ways the official terms exclude.
- Treat the license as a transfer of copyright: the studio still owns the images.

## In this collection

Download-only: files under this license are not committed; wallman keeps them on your disk, records where to fetch them, and never adds them to git. The official terms are about using the stills, not about republishing a collection of them, so wallman does not publish copies.
