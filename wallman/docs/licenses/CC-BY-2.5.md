You can copy, share and adapt the work for any purpose, even commercially, as long as you credit the author.

- Adapt it: crop, remix, transform, build on it.
- Use it for any purpose, including commercial use.

## You must

- Credit the original author (and any party they name), with the title and a link to the source if one is given.
- Keep the copyright notice and give a link to, or a copy of, the license.
- Say that your version is derived from the original when you publish an adaptation.
- Remove the credit if the author asks you to.

- Use it in a way the license does not cover (rights beyond copyright, such as privacy or personality rights, are not granted).
