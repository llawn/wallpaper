# GNU General Public License 2.0 only (GPL-2.0-only)

## In short

A copyleft license, written for software: you may share and change the work, but everything you distribute that is based on it must be released under the same license, with its source. This entry means exactly version 2.0, not later ones.

## You may

- Copy and share the work, modified or not, for any purpose, including commercially.
- Change it and publish your changes.

## You must

- Keep the copyright and license notices, and give recipients a copy of the license.
- Release adaptations under the same license.
- Make the preferred form for modification available (for an image: the editable source, such as layered files, not only the exported picture).
- Carry a prominent notice in changed files saying that you changed them, with the date.

## You cannot

- Add restrictions that the license does not have to what you distribute.
- Distribute a derived work under a different license.

## In this collection

Committed to git: files under this license may be committed to the git repository. wallman allows `Add to git` for them, because the license permits republishing copies as long as you follow the conditions above. The GPL is unusual for artwork; the entry's author is the one who chose it.
