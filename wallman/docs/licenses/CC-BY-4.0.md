# Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

## In short

You can copy, share and adapt the work for any purpose, even commercially, as long as you credit the author.

## You may

- Copy and redistribute the work in any medium or format.
- Adapt it: crop, remix, transform, build on it.
- Use it for any purpose, including commercial use.

## You must

- Give appropriate credit: the author's name, a link to the license, and a link to the source if one is given.
- Indicate whether you made changes (a crop counts).
- Keep the copyright notice and the license notice with every copy.

## You cannot

- Use it in a way the license does not cover (rights beyond copyright, such as privacy or personality rights, are not granted).
- Suggest that the author endorses you or your use of the work.
- Add legal terms or technical measures that stop others from doing what the license allows.
