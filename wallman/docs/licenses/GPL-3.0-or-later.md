## In short

A copyleft license, written for software: you may share and change the work, but everything you distribute that is based on it must be released under the same license, with its source. This entry means version 3.0 or, at your choice, any later version.

## You may

- Copy and share the work, modified or not, for any purpose, including commercially.
- Change it and publish your changes.

## You must

- Keep the copyright and license notices, and give recipients a copy of the license.
- Release adaptations under the same license.
- Make the preferred form for modification available (for an image: the editable source, such as layered files, not only the exported picture).
- State that you changed the work, with a date.

## You cannot

- Add restrictions that the license does not have to what you distribute.
- Distribute a derived work under a different license unless it is a later version of the GPL.
