# GNU Lesser General Public License 3.0 or later (LGPL-3.0-or-later)

## In short

A weaker copyleft license, written for software libraries: you may share and change the work, and changes to the work itself stay under the license. This entry means version 3 or any later version.

## You may

- Copy and share the work, modified or not, for any purpose, including commercially.
- Combine it with other works that have different terms, as the license describes.

## You must

- Keep the copyright and license notices, and give recipients a copy of the license.
- Release changes to the work itself under the same license (or the matching GPL, as the license allows).
- Let users of a combined work replace or modify the LGPL part and reverse-engineer the combination for debugging, as the license describes.
- Make the preferred form for modification available (for an image: the editable source).

## You cannot

- Add restrictions that the license does not have to the work itself.
- Distribute a changed version of the work under a different license, other than the matching GPL.

## In this collection

Committed to git: files under this license may be committed to the git repository. wallman allows `Add to git` for them, because the license permits republishing copies as long as you follow the conditions above. The LGPL is unusual for artwork; the entry's author is the one who chose it.
