# Unsplash License

## In short

Photos from Unsplash are free to use, for commercial and non-commercial purposes, without asking or crediting. Unsplash's license is its own, not a Creative Commons license, and it has two limits: no selling the photos unaltered, and no compiling them into a competing service.

## You may

- Download, copy, modify and use the photos for free, including commercially.
- Use them without asking the photographer or Unsplash.

## You must

- Nothing. Crediting the photographer and Unsplash is appreciated, not required.

## You cannot

- Sell the photos without adding significant changes of your own.
- Compile photos from Unsplash to build a similar or competing service.
- Rely on the license for recognisable people, property or brands in the photo: those can have separate rights.

## In this collection

Download-only: files under this license are not committed; wallman keeps them on your disk, records where to fetch them, and never adds them to git. The license itself only forbids selling the photos without significant changes and compiling them to replicate a similar or competing service; keeping them out of git is wallman's conservative policy, since a repository of them could look like such a collection.
