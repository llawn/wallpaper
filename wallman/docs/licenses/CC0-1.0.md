# Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 1.0)

## In short

The author gave up their rights in the work as far as the law allows. You can do anything with it, without asking or crediting.

## You may

- Copy, modify, distribute and perform the work, even commercially.
- Do all of that without asking permission.

## You must

- Nothing. Crediting the author is polite but not required.

## You cannot

- Rely on the waiver for rights it does not touch: patent and trademark rights are not affected, and neither are other people's rights in the work (for example a person's image).
- Expect any warranty: the work comes as it is.
